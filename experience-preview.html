Original story IP

Noura & Sami and the Missing Basket

Noura counted four baskets at the little market. The sign said they needed five. One basket was missing. The class follows the clues, practices positional language, counts together and finishes with a social lesson about thanking helpers.

The story is not decoration. It connects literacy, numeracy, role-play and social-emotional learning inside the same week.
